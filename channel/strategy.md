# AI Daily Drop: strategy and timetable

## Goal
Grow a faceless AI tools & tech news channel to YouTube Partner Program as fast as possible.
Current YPP entry (until 31 Jan 2027): 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours (12 months) or 10M Shorts views (90 days).
From 1 Feb 2027 new applicants need 8,000 watch hours (365 days) or 20M Shorts views (90 days), so speed matters.

## Weekly timetable (one Short per day)
| Day | Pillar | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | New AI tool you can use today | "This free AI turns photos into 3D" |
| Tue | Big AI news, explained simply | "OpenAI just raised $11B: here's why" |
| Wed | Free AI that replaces a paid app | "Stop paying for Canva: try this" |
| Thu | AI news / big company move | "Google just turned off AI for students" |
| Fri | 3 AI tools you didn't know | quick-fire list, 3 tools in 40 s |
| Sat | AI tip / how-to in 40 s | "Make ChatGPT write like you" |
| Sun | Weekly recap: top 3 AI stories | "3 AI stories you missed this week" |

## Posting slots (Asia/Karachi)
- Default: **16:00 PKT** every day (Metricool's best-time data for YouTube peaks 15:00-17:00 PKT on weekdays).
- Test: in weeks 1-2, alternate 16:00 and 21:00 PKT (21:00 PKT = 11:00 New York) to see which works for this channel;
  write the result in learnings.md and then use the winner.
- The daily run starts mid-day, so the video is ready well before its slot.

## What a "never scroll" Short looks like
- Hook in the first 1-2 seconds, footage moving from frame 1, captions on screen from the first word.
- A new shot every 2-3 s, push-in zooms, flash + whoosh on cuts (engine does this).
- One surprising number or "you can now..." payoff early. 30-45 s. A question at the end for comments.

## Growth levers to try over time (record results in learnings.md)
- Hook styles: shock-claim, question, you-can-now, number, vs, warning.
- Topics: ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude features vs new startup tools vs phones/hardware vs money stories.
- Voices: 3 rotating; if one clearly wins over 10+ videos, use it more.
- Later: one 6-10 min long video per week (watch hours for YPP), and posting the same Shorts to TikTok/Instagram via Metricool.
