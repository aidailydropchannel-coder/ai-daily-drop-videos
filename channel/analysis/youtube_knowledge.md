## 2026-11-25 - Shorts viewers -> subscribers
- Benchmarks for subscribers per Shorts view disagree a lot and none come from YouTube itself: 0.05-0.5%
  (creatortribune), 0.3-0.8% (owlclaw, citing vidIQ/TubeBuddy 2025), ~1.7% (virvid, citing Voomo). Long-form ~1-2%.
  Our channel: ~11 subs / ~2,350 views = ~0.5% (inside the normal Shorts range; the problem is reach, not conversion).
- Numbered series convert better (virvid cites Swydo 2025: +67%; secondary source, treat as direction only).
- A specific end CTA ("follow for Free AI Tool #2 next Tuesday") beats a generic "subscribe" (opinion on all pages, no data).
- YouTube's own feature: **Related video** on a Short puts a tappable link to one of our own videos under the Short
  (YouTube Help 14075157). It can only be set in YouTube Studio / the YouTube app, not by Metricool. Our first long video
  converted 2 subs from 37 views (one video = signal only), so pointing Shorts at the weekly long video is the cheapest
  sub + watch-hour lever we have.
- Since Aug 2026 YouTube counts views from the first frame on all formats, so views/subscriber ratios from older
  articles are not comparable. Use engaged views for comparisons.
Sources: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157 ; https://virvid.ai/blog/turning-shorts-views-into-subscribers-2026 ;
https://owlclaw.com/benchmarks/youtube-shorts-benchmarks/ ; https://creatortribune.com/convert-views-to-subscribers/ ;
https://unityfilms.net/youtube-shorts-analytics/

## 2026-11-28 - Shorts first second / swipe-away benchmarks (evidence: weak)
- No YouTube-official number for a "good" viewed-vs-swiped rate was found. Marketing blogs quote "under 30% swiped away"
  and "70%+ average viewed" as healthy for 2026 (unityfilms.net); its one cited claim (Shorts under 30% swipe-away get
  ~4x the sustained distribution of those over 50%, "Tubular") links only to a homepage, so treat it as unverified.
- A 2026 academic dataset of 92,654 Shorts from 345 Western creators (YTShorts-92K, arXiv 2611.13002) found only weak
  linear links between simple content features and views: on-screen text r = -0.08, hashtag count r = -0.06,
  duration r = -0.05. It has no retention data. Takeaway: surface features (text, hashtags, length alone) do not
  decide reach; what viewers do in the first seconds does - which only Studio's "viewed vs swiped" shows.
- Our own data is the stronger guide here: shorter Shorts reach a higher % viewed (pattern over 13 videos, see
  analysis/2026-11-28.md). Connecting the vidIQ browser extension would unlock per-Short "stayed to watch".
Sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2611.13002 ; https://unityfilms.net/youtube-shorts-analytics/
