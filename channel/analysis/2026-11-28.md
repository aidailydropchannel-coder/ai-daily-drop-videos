## Diagnosis
1. **Biggest problem (unchanged): reach per Short.** Every Short since 006 stops at 14-150 views. YouTube widens reach
   when "viewed %" is high; ours is 38-65%, under the ~70% that is usually quoted as "pushed".
2. **Pattern (13 Shorts): shorter Shorts are watched further.**
   <= 27 s (007, 008, 012, 013): avg 56% viewed. 28-29 s (002, 005, 006, 011): 48%. 31-37 s (001, 003, 004, 009, 010): 43%.
   The new best, 012 (25.1 s), has the highest viewed % of any Short with real views (65%). -> target 22-25 s.
3. **Frame check (0 s / 1 s / 3 s of 012, 013, 014, 015).** Brand + benefit in the first second is now on screen (014/015
   have the solid pill). New problem from the 25 Nov brightness lift: on bright footage the cyan accent line had no dark
   edge and washed out ("ChatGPT" on 012 frame 0 was hard to read; "your deals" on 015 faded over a face). Fixed in the engine.
   013's opening line was a question with no brand ("You on video, no camera" / "Want a video of you talking...");
   it had the lowest engaged % of the new ones (17%). Signal (1 video), consistent with the 25 Nov brand-first rule.
4. **Outliers in our niche (signal, never copy):** small channels breaking out this month use (a) one specific free product
   per Short with a concrete result in the title ("... turn anything you build in chat into real desktop software. Free on
   Mac and Windows." 11.4K views, 31 s; we had that same Claude story only as item 3 of 014), (b) "what happens when you
   ask ChatGPT to..." experiments, (c) a use case for a group (students' exams, job interviews, office workers). Most are
   31-60 s with a face on camera, so length there is not comparable to our faceless format.
5. Series: 012 "Free AI Tool #1" got 126 views / 65% viewed vs 013 (no series) 69 / 56%. One pair = signal only.
6. Long video L001: 64 views, 107 s average, 2 subs. Still the best sub converter per view. The Sunday task that should make
